weather_shark live demo

A radar viewer for tracking tornadoes, written in Rust with egui. It shows real NEXRAD Level II data on a chase map with roads and towns, and can stream NOAA Weather Radio. This is the real app, compiled to WebAssembly.

How it works

  • Radar volumes come straight from NOAA's public NEXRAD archive on AWS. A small decoder in Rust unpacks the bzip2 records and reads the Level II message 31 radials.
  • Each sweep is drawn to a texture once, then panned and zoomed on the GPU. Panes split in a fibonacci spiral like a tiling window manager, and all share one view and one crosshair, so a velocity couplet lines up with the hook echo and any debris signature in CC.
  • Roads, highway numbers and towns are OpenStreetMap vector tiles from OpenFreeMap, decoded by a small protobuf reader written for this.
  • Settings, colour tables and alert sounds live in a Lua file. It runs in piccolo, a Lua VM written in pure Rust, so the same config works on the desktop and here.
  • Active NWS warnings are checked every minute. A confirmed tornado sounds a siren, and every rule can be changed in the config. Your browser only allows sound after you click the page once.
